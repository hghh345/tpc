the tiny photo club faq

questions, questions.

a few things you might be wondering.

what exactly am i getting?

you'll get either 12 or 36 little physical photo prints from your summer.

each print is 10 × 10 cm, with your photo printed as an 8 × 8 cm square with a white border, on thick matte paper.

how does it work?

first, choose your package: 12 or 36 photos.

then upload all the photos you're considering. you don't have to choose them right away...think of it as your little photo bank.

once you've narrowed it down, pick the ones you want to print, pay, and we'll take care of the rest.

i don't know what to print.

that's kind of the point.

print the blurry one. the photo of someone's hand. the terrible restaurant lighting. your friend's face when they weren't posing. the view from the train. the thing you saw on a walk. the photo you forgot you took.

you don't need 12 beautiful photos. you just need 12 things you want to remember.

what can i upload?

JPG, JPEG, PNG, and HEIC.

upload as many photos as you want while you're making your photo bank. you'll choose your final 12 or 36 afterwards.

will my photos be cropped?

yes ♡

your photos are printed square, so photos with different proportions will be cropped to fit.

we don't currently have a crop editor, so keep the square format in mind when choosing your photos.

can i print the same photo more than once?

absolutely.

if one photo deserves two copies, click it twice. if it deserves twelve, we're not here to judge.

what happens to my photos?

your photos are uploaded securely and used to create your prints.

we don't sell your photos or use them for advertising.

how do i get my prints?

for this summer experiment, there are three ways to get your photos:

free pickup at an art gallery in Santos, only until Oct 22nd.
free entry to No Small Photos on Oct 4th, in the park.
or €2 mail anywhere in Portugal.

you'll choose how you'd like to get them before you pay.

when will i get my prints?

this is a small experiment, so we're keeping the print schedule small too.

gallery pickup is available until Oct 22nd. if you're getting your photos by mail, we'll send them out once they're ready.

what if i want to change my photos?

you can take your time while you're building your photo bank.

once you've submitted your final selection and paid, we can't guarantee that changes can be made.

so take your time. that's what the photo pile is for ♡

is this a subscription?

not this time.

the summer experiment is a one-time purchase. we're trying something old-fashioned: printing some photos, giving them to real people, and seeing what happens.

who is behind this?

Tiny Photo Club is a funfunfun production, part of funfunfun.events .

funfunfun is about creating tiny portals into big fun — little experiences, gatherings, and experiments that get people out of their usual routines and into something a little more curious.

Tiny Photo Club is an extension of that idea.

because sometimes a tiny photo can be a portal too.
