the tiny photo club faq

questions, questions.

a few things you might be wondering.

what exactly am i getting?

twelve little physical photo prints every month.

each print is 10 × 10 cm, with your photo printed as an 8 × 8 cm square with a white border, on thick matte paper.

how does it work?

choose your plan, upload the photos you're considering, then pick your dozen.

we print them and get them into the physical world.

will my photos be cropped?

yes ♡

your photos are printed square, so photos with different proportions will be cropped to fit.

we don't currently have a crop editor, so keep the square format in mind when choosing your photos.

can i print the same photo more than once?

absolutely.

if one photo deserves two copies, click it twice. if it deserves twelve, we're not here to judge.

what happens to my photos?

your photos are used to create your prints and are stored securely while your order is being fulfilled.

we don't sell your photos or use them for advertising.

when will i get my prints?

your prints are made after you submit your dozen.

delivery or pickup timing depends on where you are and the current print schedule.

can i cancel?

yes.

you can cancel your subscription before your next billing cycle. your already-paid-for print cycle will still be fulfilled.

what if i want to change my photos?

once your dozen has been submitted for printing, we can't guarantee that changes can be made.

so take your time choosing. that's what the photo pile is for ♡

do you ship?

we're starting small.

pickup and delivery options depend on the city, so you'll see the available options when you join.

is this a gift?

it can be.

you could give someone a subscription, or just give them twelve tiny pictures of your favorite memories together.

come see what you've got.
