```html about • tiny photo club
about tiny photo club

hello, digital hoarder.

you probably have a lot of photos.

like... a lot.

47,000 photos.
screenshots of things you forgot why you saved.
three nearly identical pictures of the same sunset.
a camera roll full of moments you swear you'll look at someday.

we're all digital hoarders.

and honestly?
we think that's kind of beautiful.

but some memories deserve to exist somewhere other than your phone.

that's why we made tiny photo club.

every month, you pick twelve photos.
we turn them into twelve little prints
and send them into the physical world.

something to put on your wall.
something to tuck into a drawer.
something to give to someone.
something to find years from now.

nothing complicated.
just your photos,
off your camera roll
and into your hands.

keep the memories.
lose the scroll.

pick your dozen →

tiny photo club ♡

```
